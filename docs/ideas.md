# Ideas

Future use cases to revisit. None of these are committed, and some may not belong in Pitch In at all.

## Same approach, different events

Pitch In is a calendar of drop-in volunteering events. The same approach (a curated, regularly updated calendar of recurring local events, starting with South Australia) could suit other kinds of event:

- **Free yoga and exercise:** free classes and groups, such as outdoor yoga, park runs and community fitness sessions.
- **Festive events:** Christmas pageants, carols and other seasonal community events.

## Open questions

- Is this a new product, or an extension of Pitch In? Pitch In is specifically about volunteering, so these may be a separate project that reuses the data pipeline.
- If separate, how much of the stack can be shared (the event-series format, the generator and the frontend)?
